- Do not use em dashes. Use commas, parentheses, or separate sentences instead.
- Use plain, direct language. Avoid buzzwords and clichés (for example "synergy", "rockstar", "ninja", "guru").
- Prefer American English spelling.
- Keep each bullet concise, ideally to a single line.
- Lead bullets with a strong past-tense verb (for example "Built", "Led", "Cut").
